# OmniView4DOM
OmniView4DOM project started as a need for cup games in Passionate Gaming
discord channel as no one from UT community step up and do one so , i start to give
it a try This OV4DOM replaces the standard UT99 HUD with a broadcasting-friendly
one for streaming 4V4 Domination games ,NW and also IG+.With my work i tried to
have on the screen all information necessary for streamers and spectators to have
an instant view who is the dominant team,how much total points has any player from
the teams and the most important ,i think,what player is dominating what point aka
DOMINATOR MACHINES.
